# EMAIL COPY GENIUS

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Production Ready
**Dependencies:** Market Intelligence Synthesizer, Offer Architect, Sales Page Copywriter

---

## INTENT

Create high-converting email campaigns and sequences that build authentic relationships, deliver consistent value, and drive transformation-focused action through strategic copywriting and sequencing.

---

## WHEN TO USE

- Creating welcome sequences for new subscribers
- Building launch campaigns for product releases
- Designing nurture sequences for ongoing engagement
- Writing sales emails that convert without manipulation
- Crafting reactivation campaigns for cold lists
- Developing segmentation and personalization strategies
- Optimizing email performance (opens, clicks, conversions)

## NOT FOR

- One-off transactional emails (receipts, confirmations)
- Internal team communications
- Social media copywriting (different medium, different skill)
- SMS/text message campaigns (different constraints)

---

## QUICK REFERENCE

### Golden Rules

1. **One Idea, One CTA:** Every email has singular focus and clear next step
2. **Relationship Before Revenue:** Build trust through value before asking for sale
3. **Curiosity Without Clickbait:** Subject lines intrigue honestly, don't deceive
4. **Story Over Pitch:** Narrative-driven emails outperform feature lists
5. **Test and Iterate:** Open rates, click rates, and conversions guide refinement

### Fast Example

**Scenario:** New subscriber from lead magnet (cold traffic)

**Process:** Apply 5-email Welcome Sequence framework
1. Email 1: Who you are, what to expect, first value delivery
2. Email 2: Origin story or "why I care"
3. Email 3: Quick Win framework
4. Email 4: Social proof and community invitation
5. Email 5: Soft intro to core offer

**Result:** Subscriber warmed to list, understands value, ready for future offers

## CORE FRAMEWORKS

### Framework 1: Relationship Ladder Sequence

**Purpose:** Move subscribers from stranger to customer through value-driven progression

**When to Use:** Any email sequence (welcome, nurture, launch)

**Inputs:**
- Subscriber source (lead magnet, quiz, webinar, etc.)
- Avatar details (pain points, desires, language)
- Mechanism/method you teach
- Core offer (if applicable)

**Outputs:**
- Email sequence (5-12 emails depending on temperature)
- Subject lines for each email
- Clear progression from value to invitation

**Procedure:**

1. **Assess List Temperature**
   - Cold traffic (ads, SEO): 10-14 emails before first pitch
   - Warm traffic (referral, webinar): 5-7 emails before first pitch
   - Hot traffic (application, high-intent): 3-5 emails before pitch

2. **Map Relationship Progression**
   - Intro: Who you are, what to expect
   - Value: Educational content, frameworks, insights
   - Story: Origin story, why you care about this
   - Mechanism: Introduce unique method/approach
   - Proof: Testimonials, case studies, results
   - Invitation: Present offer naturally
   - Urgency: Real deadline or scarcity (if applicable)

3. **Write Email Sequence**
   - Start with intro email (set expectations)
   - Deliver value in 3-5 emails (no asks)
   - Introduce mechanism/story
   - Share proof
   - Make invitation (benefit-focused CTA)
   - Follow up with urgency only if real

4. **Apply Email Architecture** (each email)
   - Subject: Curiosity without clickbait (60 chars max)
   - Preview: Extends subject line curiosity (100 chars max)
   - Opening: Hook referencing previous email or promised content
   - Body: Scannable, singular focus, natural voice
   - CTA: Clear next step, benefit-focused
   - P.S.: Reinforce main point or bonus insight

5. **Sequence Testing**
   - Track: Open rates (target >20%), click rates (target >2-5%)
   - Identify: Which email has drop-off
   - Refine: Subject lines, hooks, CTAs based on data

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Open rates >20% maintained through sequence
- [ ] Click rates >2% on emails with CTA
- [ ] Conversion rate aligns with avatar temperature
- [ ] No anti-patterns (manipulation, vague promises, feature dumps)
- [ ] Passes Heart Test

### Framework 2: Sideways Sales Letter

**Purpose:** Multi-email narrative that drives opens through story while naturally integrating offer

**When to Use:** Launch campaigns, high-value offer promotions, reactivation sequences

**Inputs:**
- Core transformation story (customer or your own)
- Mechanism (what makes your approach unique)
- Offer details
- Timeline (how many days for sequence)

**Outputs:**
- Multi-email story arc (typically 5-8 emails)
- Cliffhangers and open loops between emails
- Natural offer integration within narrative

**Procedure:**

1. **Identify Story Arc**
   - Setup: Before state (struggle, pain, failed attempts)
   - Catalyst: What changed or what they tried differently
   - Breakthrough: First result or moment of clarity
   - Transformation: How life is different now
   - Invitation: How reader can experience same transformation

2. **Break Story Across Emails**
   - Email 1: Setup (the struggle)
   - Email 2: Failed solutions (what didn't work)
   - Email 3: Catalyst (open loop: "Then I discovered...")
   - Email 4: Mechanism reveal (what makes this different)
   - Email 5: Breakthrough (the result)
   - Email 6: Proof (others who've done same)
   - Email 7: Invitation (offer presentation)
   - Email 8: Urgency (real deadline)

3. **Create Open Loops**
   - Each email ends with curiosity trigger
   - Next email continues story where left off
   - Drives opens through narrative momentum

4. **Integrate Offer Naturally**
   - Don't interrupt story to pitch
   - Offer emerges as natural solution to story problem
   - Mechanism (your unique method) is the bridge

5. **Test Story Engagement**
   - Open rates should increase through sequence (momentum)
   - If drop-off, story lost engagement (refine hook)

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Open rates increase or maintain through sequence
- [ ] Story feels cohesive (not disjointed emails)
- [ ] Offer integration feels natural (not jarring)
- [ ] Mechanism clearly explained
- [ ] Passes Heart Test (authentic story, not manufactured)

### Framework 3: Email Architecture Formula

**Purpose:** Structure individual emails for maximum clarity, engagement, and action

**When to Use:** Every email in every sequence

**Procedure:**

1. **Subject Line (60 chars max)**
   - Formulas:
     - Curiosity gap: "The [surprising thing] about [familiar topic]"
     - Benefit-driven: "How to [desired outcome] without [common pain]"
     - Story hook: "[Unexpected event] taught me [insight]"
     - Question: "Ready to [transformation]?"
     - Numbered: "[Number] ways to [outcome]"
   - Test: Does it intrigue without deceiving?

2. **Preview Text (100 chars max)**
   - Extends subject line curiosity
   - Completes the thought or adds context
   - Don't waste on "View in browser" or "Click here"

3. **Opening Hook**
   - Reference previous email ("Yesterday I shared...")
   - Ask compelling question
   - Make bold statement
   - Share surprising fact
   - Must stop scroll and engage

4. **Body Structure**
   - Short paragraphs (2-4 sentences max)
   - White space for scannability
   - Subheadings if longer email
   - One core idea (don't wander)
   - Natural, conversational voice

5. **CTA (Call to Action)**
   - One clear next step
   - Benefit-focused language ("Get the framework" not "Click here")
   - Standalone button or linked text
   - Reinforced in P.S.

6. **P.S. (Postscript)**
   - Reinforce main CTA, or
   - Add bonus insight, or
   - Create curiosity for next email

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Subject line <60 characters, intriguing
- [ ] Opening hooks reader immediately
- [ ] Body scannable, single focus
- [ ] CTA clear and benefit-driven
- [ ] P.S. adds value or reinforcement

### Framework 4: Launch Sequence Framework

**Purpose:** Coordinated email campaign for product launch (cart open to close)

**When to Use:** New product launches, cohort openings, limited offers

**Inputs:**
- Product details (transformation, mechanism, price, timeline)
- Launch dates (pre-launch, cart open, cart close)
- Avatar objections and desires
- Social proof available

**Outputs:**
- Complete launch email sequence (8-12 emails)
- Pre-launch seed content
- Open cart emails (invitation, mechanism, proof, FAQ)
- Close cart emails (urgency, final call)

**Procedure:**

1. **Pre-Launch Phase (3-5 emails, 5-7 days before cart open)**
   - Seed content related to mechanism
   - Build curiosity without revealing offer
   - Prime subscribers for problem/solution fit
   - Examples:
     - "The mistake most people make with [topic]"
     - "Why [common approach] doesn't work"
     - "The mechanism that changes everything"

2. **Cart Open Phase (3-4 emails, days 1-3 of open cart)**
   - Email 1: Invitation (transformation, who it's for)
   - Email 2: Mechanism deep-dive (what makes this different)
   - Email 3: Proof (testimonials, case studies, results)
   - Email 4: FAQ (handle objections, build belief)

3. **Mid-Cart Phase (2-3 emails, days 4-6)**
   - Reminder (for those who haven't acted)
   - Additional proof or bonus reveal
   - Behind-the-scenes or origin story

4. **Close Cart Phase (2-3 emails, final 24-48 hours)**
   - Urgency email (real deadline, why it matters)
   - Scarcity (if genuine limit exists)
   - Final call (last chance, reinforce transformation)

5. **Post-Launch**
   - Thank you (for buyers: welcome, onboarding preview)
   - Re-engagement (for non-buyers: value continues, stay tuned)

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Pre-launch builds curiosity without revealing offer
- [ ] Cart open clearly presents transformation + mechanism
- [ ] Proof addresses skepticism
- [ ] FAQ handles objections
- [ ] Urgency real (not manufactured)
- [ ] Conversion aligns with avatar temperature and offer quality

### Framework 5: Subject Line Formulas

**Purpose:** Maximize open rates with honest curiosity and relevance

**When to Use:** Every email

**Formulas:**

1. **Curiosity Gap**
   - "The [surprising thing] about [familiar topic]"
   - "What [unexpected group] knows about [topic]"
   - "The real reason [outcome] happens"

2. **Benefit-Driven**
   - "How to [desired outcome] without [common pain]"
   - "[Number] ways to [transformation]"
   - "Get [result] in [timeframe]"

3. **Story Hook**
   - "[Unexpected event] taught me [insight]"
   - "I never thought [X] until [Y]"
   - "This [experience] changed everything"

4. **Question (Assumes "Yes")**
   - "Ready to [transformation]?"
   - "Want to [desired outcome]?"
   - "Tired of [pain point]?"

5. **Numbered List**
   - "[Number] mistakes with [topic]"
   - "[Number] secrets to [outcome]"
   - "[Number] reasons [insight]"

6. **Personalization**
   - "[Name], here's what I'd do"
   - "Your [milestone] breakthrough"
   - "For [avatar]: [specific benefit]"

**Testing:**
- A/B test subject lines (minimum 100 per variant)
- Track open rate by formula type
- Refine based on avatar response
- Avoid: ALL CAPS, excessive punctuation!!!, emoji spam, clickbait

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Open rate >20% (good), >30% (excellent)
- [ ] Subject line honest (no deception)
- [ ] Relevant to email content (matches expectation)
- [ ] <60 characters (mobile preview)

### Framework 6: List Segmentation Strategy

**Purpose:** Deliver personalized content based on subscriber behavior and characteristics

**When to Use:** Growing lists (500+ subscribers), diverse avatars, multiple offers

**Inputs:**
- Subscriber data (source, quiz results, behavior)
- Engagement tracking (opens, clicks, page visits)
- Purchase history
- Milestone progress (if in program)

**Outputs:**
- Segmented email paths
- Personalized content by segment
- Targeted offers based on fit

**Segmentation Types:**

1. **Behavioral Segmentation**
   - Openers vs non-openers
   - Clickers vs readers
   - Page visitors (high intent)
   - Cart abandoners

2. **Engagement-Based**
   - Hot (opened 3+ of last 5 emails)
   - Warm (opened 1-2 of last 5 emails)
   - Cold (opened 0 of last 5 emails)

3. **Avatar-Based**
   - Quiz results or intake data
   - Niche or industry
   - Experience level
   - Goals or pain points

4. **Journey-Based**
   - Where they are in program (milestone tracking)
   - Product owned
   - Upsell readiness

**Procedure:**

1. **Tag Subscribers**
   - Capture data at opt-in (quiz, intake form)
   - Track behavior (email opens, link clicks, page visits)
   - Update tags based on engagement

2. **Create Segment Paths**
   - Hot leads: Shorter sequences, faster to offer
   - Cold leads: More value, slower to pitch
   - Avatar A: Content specific to their pain points
   - Avatar B: Different examples, same mechanism

3. **Personalize Content**
   - Use merge tags for name, details
   - Reference their specific situation
   - Adjust examples to their niche

4. **Test and Optimize**
   - Compare segment performance
   - Identify which segments convert best
   - Refine segmentation criteria

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Segmentation increases relevance (higher engagement)
- [ ] Personalized paths outperform one-size-fits-all
- [ ] Conversion rates improve with targeting
- [ ] Segments manageable (don't over-complicate)

## HEURISTICS

**Decision Tree:**

- IF subscriber source = cold traffic THEN welcome sequence = 7-10 emails before first pitch
- IF subscriber source = warm/referred THEN welcome sequence = 5 emails before pitch
- IF open rate drops below 15% THEN add pattern interrupt in next email (story, question, bold statement)
- IF launching to cold list THEN add 2-3 extra value emails in pre-launch phase
- IF list hasn't heard from you in 30+ days THEN start with re-engagement sequence ("Still want to hear from me?")
- IF someone clicks but doesn't buy THEN segment into objection-handling nurture
- IF subject line gets <15% opens THEN test new formula (too boring or too clickbaity)
- IF email longer than 3 screens THEN break into multiple emails or tighten ruthlessly
- IF someone opens 5+ emails but never clicks THEN offer misaligned OR CTA weak
- IF conversion rate below expectations THEN check: avatar fit, mechanism clarity, proof quality, objections addressed
