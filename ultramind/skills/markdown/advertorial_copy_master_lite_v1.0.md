# Advertorial Copy Master Skill (LITE)
## "The Story-Selling Strategist" — Patch v1.0

### Intent
Transform **cold traffic** into **warm buying intent** via **educational, story-driven** advertorials that feel like *discovery*, not selling.

### Prime Directive
**Trust is the ultimate aim of persuasion.**
Sell **ideas + beliefs** first → then product.

### What This Skill Is (and isn't)
- **IS:** Editorial-style bridge content (news/blog/discovery story/exposé/listicle) that sells the **solution mechanism**.
- **IS NOT:** A sales page (product page sells the product; advertorial sells the *why/how/hope*).

---

## Inputs Required (No Guessing)
**From Market Intelligence Synthesizer**
- Avatar + exact VoC language
- pains, desires, beliefs to shift
- objections + skepticism triggers
- proof sources available (testimonials, studies, experts, demos)

**From Offer Architect**
- chosen angle + hooks
- UMP / UMS statement candidates
- destination CTA (sales page / VSL / product page)
- compliance constraints + platform constraints

If inputs are missing → label **UNKNOWN** and request them in-line.

---

## Core Structure (Universal Narrative Spine)

1) **Pattern Interrupt (Hook)**
2) **Empathy & Validation (Align)** ("It's not your fault… you were missing one piece.")
3) **Discovery Story (Epiphany)** (crisis → search → failures → unexpected clue → insight)
4) **UMP: Unique Mechanism of Problem** (real root cause; why past attempts failed)
5) **UMS: Unique Mechanism of Solution** (how the solution addresses UMP; differentiator)
6) **Soft Close** (value → risk reversal → reasonable urgency → clear CTA)

---

## RMBC Workflow (Stefan Georgi Style)

### R — Research
Implement provided VoC, objections, proof. **Do not invent.**

### M — Mechanism (the heart)

**UMP requirements**
- counterintuitive but instantly believable
- 6th-grade explanation + metaphor
- explains past failures
- connects to daily experience

**UMS requirements**
- directly solves UMP (not "better ingredients")
- explains HOW it works
- differentiates from alternatives
- can be named (optional)

**Mechanism Flow:**
Problem pain → UMP reveal → why common solutions fail → UMS reveal → product as delivery vehicle → proof → close.

- 1–3 sentence paragraphs

- strategic subheads + bolding
- abundant white space

## Format Selector (Pick ONE)

1) **Personal Discovery (2AM Revelation)** — relatability, supplements/health, social traffic
2) **Nightmare → Rescue** — crisis markets (pet, emergency, fear-driven)
3) **Whistleblower / Exposé** — distrust markets (supplements/finance/beauty)
4) **Comparison / Testing** — competitive categories ("I tested X…")
5) **Educational Listicle** — Google/search intent

---

## The Engagement Engine

Use 4–6 cycles of:
**Tension → Resolution → Redirect**
Each "resolution" opens the next question.

Open 3–5 loops early, close them progressively.

---

## Compliance Guardrails (Health/Supplements)

**✅ Safe language:**
- "may help support"
- "designed to"
- "supports healthy…"
- "promotes…"
- "could assist…"

**❌ Risky language:**
- "cures"
- "treats"
- "reverses"
- "guarantees"
- disease claims

**Platform rules:**
- No fake scarcity
- Testimonials qualified (results vary)
- No unverified stats
- No before/after in ad creative
