## Purpose

Map your unique strengths, experiences, and capabilities to resonant value offers for specific prospect personas. Ensures your outreach is grounded in authentic capability, not hype.

---

## Inputs

- **artifact.project_brief.v0_1**: Your background and strengths
- **artifact.persona_map.v0_1**: Target persona challenges

---

## Outputs

- **artifact.value_offer.v0_1**: Clear, truth-based value proposition

---

## Process

### 1. Inventory Your Strengths

Catalog your genuine, verifiable strengths across these categories:

**Experience:**
- Years in specific domain
- Companies/roles that carry credibility
- Industries you deeply understand

**Track Record:**
- Quantifiable results you've personally delivered
- Projects you've led (with real outcomes)
- Problems you've solved repeatedly

**Expertise:**
- Technical skills or frameworks you've mastered
- Methodologies you've developed or refined
- Patterns you've observed across multiple contexts

**Access:**
- Communities you're part of
- Networks you can leverage
- Resources you can provide

**Examples:**
- "10 years building ML systems at Google"
- "Led 50+ SaaS implementations, average ARR growth 140%"
- "Created the 'Retention Ladder' framework (used by 200+ companies)"
- "Active in YC founder community, can make warm intros"

### 2. Identify Persona Pain Points

From KB-002 persona mapping, extract specific challenges:

**Ambitious Builder challenges:**
- Scaling team without slowing velocity
- Hiring senior talent in competitive market
- Maintaining culture during hypergrowth

**Careful Optimizer challenges:**
- Tool sprawl and data fragmentation
- Proving ROI of new initiatives
- Process efficiency at scale

**Visionary Explorer challenges:**
- Staying ahead of trends
- Building innovative features with limited resources
- Gaining executive buy-in for experiments

**Collaborative Connector challenges:**
- Building engaged communities
- Facilitating meaningful connections
- Measuring community value

### 3. Map Strengths to Pain Points

Create **Strength → Outcome → Pain Solved** connections:

**Formula:**
```
[Your Strength] enables [Specific Outcome] which solves [Their Pain]
```

**Example 1:**
- Strength: "Built ML features at Google for 8 years"
- Outcome: "Ship AI features without hiring full ML team"
- Pain Solved: "Ambitious Builder can't afford $300K ML engineer"

**Example 2:**
- Strength: "Analyzed 200+ SaaS pricing pages, created optimization framework"
- Outcome: "Increase trial-to-paid conversion by 15-25%"
- Pain Solved: "Careful Optimizer needs data-driven pricing decisions"

**Example 3:**
- Strength: "Early adopter of AI tools, tested 50+ in last year"
- Outcome: "Navigate AI landscape without wasting time/money on wrong tools"
- Pain Solved: "Visionary Explorer overwhelmed by options"

### 4. Craft Your Value Offer

Using the strongest strength → pain match, structure your offer:

**Components:**
1. **Headline** (one sentence, outcome-focused)
2. **Target Outcome** (specific result)
3. **How It Works** (brief method, 2-3 sentences)
4. **Why You** (credibility, based on real strengths)
5. **Deliverable** (concrete next step)

**Template:**
```
Headline: Help [persona] [achieve outcome] without [common obstacle]

Target Outcome: [Specific measurable result]

How It Works: [Brief explanation of approach using your method/framework]

Why You: [Real credential or track record that proves capability]

Deliverable: [Specific thing they get - guide, session, framework]
```

## Examples

### Example 1: ML Engineer → SaaS Founders

**Strengths Inventory:**
- 8 years at Google building ML features
- Led 12 AI implementations post-Google
- Created "AI Readiness Ladder" framework
- Technical but can explain to non-technical folks

**Persona:** Ambitious Builder (Series A SaaS CEO)
**Pain:** Want AI features but can't hire/afford ML team

**Value Offer:**
```yaml
headline: "Help Series A SaaS teams ship AI features without hiring a full ML team"

target_outcome: "Launch your first AI feature in 30 days instead of 6 months"

how_it_works: "We provide pre-trained models, integration templates, and hands-on guidance. Your existing engineers handle implementation, I fill the knowledge gaps."

why_you: "Built ML systems at Google for 8 years + 12 post-Google implementations. I've seen every common mistake and built playbooks to avoid them."

deliverable: "30-minute AI implementation roadmap session (we'll map your use case to the fastest path to production)"
```

### Example 2: SaaS Operator → Product Leaders

**Strengths Inventory:**
- 5 years as Director of Product at high-growth SaaS
- Rebuilt analytics stack 3 times
- Created comparison framework for 50+ tools
- Data-driven, optimization-focused

**Persona:** Careful Optimizer (Director/VP of Product)
**Pain:** Drowning in tool options, need data to make decision

**Value Offer:**
```yaml
headline: "Help product teams choose the right analytics stack without months of research"

target_outcome: "Make a confident tool decision in 1 week instead of 3 months"

how_it_works: "I've rebuilt analytics stacks 3 times and evaluated 50+ tools. I'll share my comparison framework (pricing, features, setup time, integrations) and help you apply it to your context."

deliverable: "Analytics Tool Comparison Spreadsheet + 20-minute walkthrough call"

why_you: "5 years as Dir of Product at [Company]. I've made these decisions under pressure and learned what actually matters vs what sounds good in demos."
```

## Edge Cases & Warnings

### Warning 1: Don't Overstate Credentials
Exaggerating backfires. Be specific and verifiable:
- ✅ "Built 12 implementations over 2 years"
- ❌ "Industry-leading expert with thousands of implementations"

### Warning 2: Strengths Must Be Relevant
Your Stanford PhD doesn't matter if prospect needs Shopify help:
- Match strength to actual pain point
- Don't list every credential—pick the 2-3 most relevant

### Warning 3: Outcomes Must Be Realistic
Don't promise what you can't deliver:
- ✅ "Help you ship first feature in 30 days" (if you've actually done this)
- ❌ "Guarantee 10x ROI in 90 days" (you can't guarantee outcomes)

### Warning 4: "Why You" Needs Proof
Back up your claims:
- ✅ "Former Head of Growth at [recognizable company]"
- ✅ "Author of [published book/framework]"
- ✅ "Led [specific project with result]"
- ❌ "World-class expert" (says who?)

- [ ] You can actually deliver on this offer
