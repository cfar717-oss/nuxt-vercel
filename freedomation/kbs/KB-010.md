# KB-010: Metrics of Resonance (Beyond Open Rates)

**Status:** draft

**Type:** procedural

**Credibility:** medium

## Purpose

Define and track metrics that measure authentic engagement and relationship quality—not just vanity metrics like open rates and clicks. Guide data collection for meaningful resonance measurement.

---

## Inputs

- **artifact.outreach_sequence.v0_1**: Campaign data
- **artifact.invitation_script.v0_1**: Community invitation data
- Email/outreach platform analytics

---

## Outputs

- **artifact.resonance_dashboard.v0_1**: Comprehensive metrics report
- Insights on what's working / what's not
- Recommendations for optimization

## Process

### 1. Metrics Hierarchy: Vanity → Value → Resonance

**Tier 1: Vanity Metrics (Track but Don't Optimize For)**
- Messages sent
- Open rate
- Click rate
- Bounce rate

**Why track:** Baseline health check (e.g., deliverability issues)
**Why not optimize:** Doesn't measure quality or fit

**Tier 2: Value Metrics (Directionally Useful)**
- Reply rate
- Asset download rate
- Call booking rate
- Conversion rate

**Why track:** Shows engagement level
**Why not enough:** Doesn't distinguish quality of engagement

**Tier 3: Resonance Metrics (Optimize for These)**
- Thoughtful reply rate (multi-sentence, personal context)
- Question depth (specific vs generic)
- Conversation threads (multi-message exchanges)
- Referral/intro rate
- Community join rate
- Long-term engagement (90+ days)

**Why prioritize:** Measures relationship quality and alignment

### 2. Define Resonance Indicators

**Strong Resonance:**
- Prospect asks specific question about their context
- Shares personal challenge or situation
- Requests introduction to colleague or peer
- Multi-message conversation (3+ exchanges)
- Books call and shows up prepared
- Joins community and contributes within 7 days
- Makes referral or intro within 30 days

**Weak Resonance:**
- Short "thanks" or "interesting" reply
- Clicks asset but doesn't engage further
- Books call but cancels/no-shows
- Joins community but never engages

**No Resonance:**
- No reply after 5 touchpoints
- Unsubscribe
- Spam report

**Anti-Resonance:**
- Negative reply ("stop spamming")
- Complaint to platform
- Public criticism of approach

### 3. Build Your Resonance Dashboard

Track these metrics weekly/monthly:

**Outreach Volume:**
- Total sent
- Delivered (not bounced)
- Deliverability rate (%)

**Basic Engagement:**
- Opens (% of delivered)
- Clicks (% of delivered)
- Replies (% of delivered)

**Resonance Indicators:**
- Thoughtful replies (% of total replies)
  - Definition: >50 words, includes question or personal context
- Conversation threads (multi-message)
  - Track: How many led to 3+ exchanges
- Asset requests (explicit "please send")
  - Higher intent than just clicking link
- Questions asked (shows active thinking)
  - Track depth: Generic vs specific

**Conversion Indicators:**
- Calls booked (% of replies)
- Calls completed (% of booked)
- Call-to-deal rate
- Community invites sent
- Community joins (% of invites)
- Active community members (posted in 30 days)

**Long-Term Health:**
- 30-day engagement (still responding/active)
- 90-day engagement
- Referrals made
- Deals closed
- Customer satisfaction (for converted customers)

### 4. Calculate Resonance Score

**Simple Resonance Score Formula:**

```
Resonance Score =
  (Thoughtful Replies × 3) +
  (Conversation Threads × 5) +
  (Questions Asked × 2) +
  (Calls Completed × 10) +
  (Referrals × 15) +
  (Community Joins × 8)

Divided by: Total Messages Sent
```

**Benchmark:**
- Score < 0.5: Low resonance (wrong audience or poor messaging)
- Score 0.5-1.5: Decent resonance (room for improvement)
- Score 1.5-3.0: Strong resonance (you're on track)
- Score > 3.0: Exceptional resonance (amplify what's working)

### 5. Analyze by Persona & Touchpoint

Don't just track overall metrics. Segment by:

**By Persona:**
- Which persona archetype has highest resonance?
- Do Ambitious Builders respond differently than Careful Optimizers?
- Adjust targeting based on resonance by persona

**By Touchpoint:**
- Which message in sequence gets most replies?
- Does touchpoint 3 (asset offer) outperform touchpoint 1 (intro)?
- Optimize sequence based on per-touchpoint performance

**By Channel:**
- Email vs LinkedIn DM vs Twitter DM
- Which channel drives deeper conversations?

**By Asset Type:**
- Do guides get more requests than checklists?
- Which asset leads to more calls?

## Examples

### Example 1: Resonance Dashboard (Monthly View)

**Campaign:** AI Implementation Outbound (Series A SaaS VPs)

**Outreach Volume:**
- Sent: 50
- Delivered: 48 (96%)
- Bounced: 2

**Basic Engagement:**
- Opened: 32 (67%)
- Clicked: 18 (38%)
- Replied: 14 (29%)

**Resonance Indicators:**
- Thoughtful replies: 9 (64% of replies)
- Conversation threads (3+ messages): 6 (43% of replies)
- Questions asked: 12 (from 9 prospects)
- Asset requests: 11 (explicit "please send")

**Conversions:**
- Calls booked: 5 (36% of replies)
- Calls completed: 4 (80% of booked)
- Community invites: 6
- Community joins: 4 (67% of invites)

**Resonance Score:**
```
(9 × 3) + (6 × 5) + (12 × 2) + (4 × 10) + (0 × 15) + (4 × 8) =
27 + 30 + 24 + 40 + 0 + 32 = 153

153 / 50 = 3.06 (Exceptional Resonance)
```

**Insight:** This campaign has strong persona-message fit. Amplify this approach.

### Example 2: Persona Comparison

**Ambitious Builders (20 prospects):**
- Reply rate: 35%

- **Resonance Score: 4.2**

**Careful Optimizers (20 prospects):**
- Reply rate: 25%

- Call booking rate: 30%
- **Resonance Score: 2.1**

**Insight:** Ambitious Builders are resonating strongly with this offer. Consider creating Optimizer-specific campaign with different value angle (data/proof-focused).

### Example 3: Touchpoint Analysis

**Touchpoint 1 (Intro + Context):**

## Edge Cases & Warnings

### Warning 1: Sample Size Matters
Don't over-optimize on small data:
- < 20 messages: Too small for patterns
- 20-50: Directional insights only
- 50-100: Start to trust patterns
- 100+: Statistically meaningful

### Warning 2: Quality > Quantity Always
Would you rather:
- 100 messages, 5% reply rate, 1 thoughtful conversation → 1 resonance
- 20 messages, 40% reply rate, 8 thoughtful conversations → 8 resonance

**Answer:** Option 2. Optimize for resonance rate, not volume.

### Warning 3: Long-Term Lag
Some metrics take time:
- Calls may happen 2-4 weeks after first message
- Referrals may take months
- Don't judge a campaign after 1 week

### Warning 4: Personas Respond Differently
Don't compare across personas without context:
- Ambitious Builders reply faster, book calls quicker
- Careful Optimizers take longer but higher quality
- Neither is "better"—just different

---

## Quality Checks

Your resonance measurement is healthy if:

- [ ] Tracking both vanity and resonance metrics (but optimizing for resonance)
- [ ] Segmenting data by persona, touchpoint, channel
- [ ] Sample size is meaningful (50+ messages minimum)
- [ ] Analyzing conversation quality, not just reply rate
- [ ] Measuring long-term engagement (30/90 days)
- [ ] Using insights to iterate messaging and targeting
- [ ] Not obsessing over open rates
