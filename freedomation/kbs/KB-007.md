## Purpose

Help individuals and small teams position themselves as "AI-first" agencies or consultancies in outbound outreach—emphasizing the unique advantages of AI-augmented work (speed, efficiency, leverage) while maintaining authenticity.

- **artifact.project_brief.v0_1**: Your services and AI usage

## Outputs

- Positioning statement for outbound messaging
- "How we work differently" narrative
- Credibility builders (what makes AI-first real vs hype)

## Process

### 1. Define Your AI-First Advantage

Identify specific ways AI enhances your work:

**Speed:**
- "We ship [deliverable] in X days instead of Y weeks"
- Example: "Complete competitive analysis in 2 days vs 2 weeks"

**Quality:**
- "AI handles [tedious task], we focus on [high-value task]"
- Example: "AI drafts initial copy, we refine strategy and voice"

**Scale:**
- "Deliver [outcome] at [fraction] of traditional cost"
- Example: "Custom market research at 1/10th agency pricing"

**Leverage:**
- "Solo/small team with output of much larger team"
- Example: "Two-person team, output of 10-person agency"

### 2. Build Your Positioning Statement

**Formula:**
```
We're [your role/service] for [target persona].

Unlike traditional [competitors], we use AI to [specific advantage], which means you get [outcome] at [fraction] of cost/time, without [common sacrifice].
```

**Example 1: Content Agency**
```
We're an AI-first content studio for B2B SaaS companies.

Unlike traditional agencies, we use AI to handle research and initial drafts, which means you get publication-ready content in 3 days instead of 3 weeks, without sacrificing quality or strategic thinking.
```

**Example 2: Product Strategy Consultant**
```
I'm a product strategy consultant for Series A startups.

Unlike traditional consultants, I use AI to analyze competitors, synthesize user research, and model scenarios, which means you get a complete strategy deck in 1 week instead of 6 weeks, at 1/5 the cost.
```

### 3. Craft "How We Work Differently" Narrative

Explain your process transparently:

**Structure:**
1. What AI handles (be specific)
2. What you handle (emphasize expertise)
3. Why this combination works
4. What you explicitly don't do (manage expectations)

**Example:**
```
How we work:

AI handles:
- Competitor research and analysis
- Initial content drafts and outlines
- SEO optimization
- Data synthesis from multiple sources

You (the human expert) handle:
- Strategic direction and positioning
- Brand voice and messaging nuance
- Client relationship and context
- Final quality control and refinement

Why it works:
- AI is fast at research and pattern recognition
- Humans are better at strategy and judgment
- Combined: speed + quality instead of speed OR quality

What we don't do:
- Fully automated content (always human-reviewed)
- Generic AI outputs (we customize for your context)
- Replace your team (we augment, not replace)
```

### 4. Build AI-First Credibility

Show you're genuinely AI-first, not just using it as a buzzword:

**Proof Points:**
- Specific tools you use (ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, etc.)
- Custom workflows or prompts you've built
- Results/time savings you've achieved
- How long you've been using AI (early adopter signal)

**Examples:**
- "We've built custom GPT workflows for [specific task]"
- "Used AI tools for 18 months, iterated 50+ times on our process"
- "Our average project: 5 hours human time, 40 hours AI-assisted time"

### 5. Address Common Objections

Preempt concerns about AI-powered work:

**Objection 1: "Isn't it just generic AI content?"**
- Response: "We use AI for speed, not replacement. Every output is human-reviewed, strategically aligned, and brand-customized."

**Objection 2: "Can I just do this myself with ChatGPT?"**
- Response: "Absolutely! But most teams don't have the time to build custom workflows, iterate prompts, and QA outputs. We've spent 500+ hours perfecting our process."

**Objection 3: "What about quality?"**
- Response: "Our quality bar is higher, not lower. AI handles research and drafts, freeing us to focus on strategy and refinement. You get more strategic thinking, not less."

## Examples

### Example 1: Solo AI-First Copywriter

**Positioning:**
```
I'm an AI-first copywriter for SaaS companies.

I use AI to research competitors, analyze high-performing copy, and generate initial drafts—then I apply 10 years of conversion copywriting expertise to refine messaging and strategy.

Result: You get conversion-optimized copy in 2 days instead of 2 weeks, at 1/3 the cost of traditional agencies, without sacrificing strategic thinking.
```

**How I Work:**
```
1. You share context (product, audience, goals)
2. AI analyzes 20-30 competitor sites + top-performing copy patterns
3. AI generates 3-5 initial approaches
4. I refine the best approach with strategic positioning + conversion psychology
5. You get final copy + strategic rationale

Timeline: 48 hours from brief to final copy
Cost: $2K vs $6K+ at traditional agencies
```

**Credibility:**
```
- Using Claude + GPT-4 for 18 months
- Built custom prompts for SaaS copywriting
- Refined process over 40+ client projects
- Average time saving: 60% vs traditional approach
```

## Edge Cases & Warnings

### Warning 1: Don't Oversell AI Magic
Be honest about limitations:
- ✅ "AI speeds up research, humans ensure accuracy"
- ❌ "Our AI does everything automatically with zero human input"

### Warning 2: Don't Compete on Price Alone
Emphasize value, not just cost:
- ✅ "1/3 the cost + faster + strategic"
- ❌ "We're the cheapest because we use AI"

### Warning 3: Some Clients Don't Want AI
Be transparent. Some will prefer 100% human:
- Disclose AI usage upfront
- Explain your quality process
- Offer "AI-free" tier if needed (at higher price)

### Warning 4: AI Tools Change Fast
Don't build positioning around a specific tool:
- ✅ "AI-powered research and analysis"
- ❌ "GPT-4 exclusive content creation" (GPT-5 comes out, positioning breaks)

- [ ] No hype or exaggeration about AI capabilities
- [ ] Transparent about AI usage (not hiding it)
