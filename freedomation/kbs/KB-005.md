## Process

### 1. Digital Asset Types (Quick Reference)

| Asset Type | Best For | Creation Time | Gating? |
|-----------|---------|--------------|---------|
| **Guide** | Step-by-step how-to | 4-8 hours | Email only |
| **Primer** | Intro to complex topic | 2-4 hours | No gate |
| **Blueprint** | System/framework visual | 3-6 hours | Email only |
| **Template** | Copy-paste tool | 1-3 hours | No gate |
| **Checklist** | Audit/evaluation | 1-2 hours | No gate |
| **Comparison** | Vendor/tool selection | 2-4 hours | Email only |
| **Case Study** | Proof of concept | 3-5 hours | Email only |

### 2. Match Asset to Persona

**Ambitious Builder** (results-focused):
- ✅ Templates (fastest path to outcome)
- ✅ Checklists (actionable)
- ✅ Case studies (proof it works)
- ❌ Long theoretical guides

**Careful Optimizer** (analytical):
- ✅ Comparisons (data-driven decisions)
- ✅ Frameworks (systematic approach)
- ✅ Blueprints (comprehensive view)
- ❌ Vague overviews

**Visionary Explorer** (big-picture):
- ✅ Primers (conceptual intro)
- ✅ Frameworks (mental models)
- ✅ Trend reports (what's next)
- ❌ Tactical checklists

**Collaborative Connector** (community-focused):
- ✅ Resource collections (curated value)
- ✅ Community playbooks (how we do things)
- ✅ Collaboration templates
- ❌ Solo-focused tools

### 3. Asset Creation Framework

**Step 1: Define the Core Problem**
What specific challenge does this solve?
- ✅ "How to onboard engineers 50% faster"
- ❌ "Everything about engineering management"

**Step 2: Choose Format**
Based on persona + problem:
- Tactical problem → Template or Checklist
- Strategic problem → Guide or Blueprint
- Evaluation need → Comparison or Framework
- Proof need → Case Study

**Step 3: Create Minimal Viable Asset**
Don't over-engineer. Ship 80% solution fast:
- Guide: 5-10 pages, clear sections, examples
- Template: Google Doc or Notion page they can copy
- Checklist: Simple bulleted list (15-30 items)
- Comparison: Spreadsheet with 3-5 criteria

**Step 4: Deliver Frictionlessly**
- PDF: Easy to save and share
- Google Doc (view-only): Quick access, no download
- Notion page: Interactive, looks modern
- GitHub gist: For code/technical content

## Examples

### Example 1: Guide for Ambitious Builders

**Title:** "The 30-Day AI Implementation Roadmap for SaaS Teams"

**Structure:**
1. Introduction (problem + promise)
2. Week 1: Decision Framework (build vs buy)
3. Week 2: Vendor Evaluation (comparison matrix)
4. Week 3: Integration Planning (technical checklist)
5. Week 4: Launch & Iteration (testing framework)
6. Appendix: Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them

**Delivery:** PDF (10 pages), gated with email

**Creation time:** 6 hours
- 2 hours: Outline + structure
- 3 hours: Content writing
- 1 hour: Formatting + visuals

### Example 2: Template for Careful Optimizers

**Title:** "SaaS Analytics Tool Comparison Spreadsheet"

**Structure (Google Sheet):**
- Tab 1: Comparison matrix (tools in rows, criteria in columns)
  - Criteria: Pricing, Setup time, Features, Integrations, Support
- Tab 2: Decision framework (weighted scoring)
- Tab 3: Implementation checklist
- Tab 4: Cost calculator

**Delivery:** Google Sheet (make a copy), no gate

**Creation time:** 3 hours
- 1 hour: Research tools
- 1 hour: Build matrix
- 1 hour: Create framework + calculator

### Example 3: Primer for Visionary Explorers

**Title:** "The AI Readiness Ladder: 5 Stages from Exploring to Production"

**Structure:**
1. Why most AI projects fail (the gap)
2. The 5 stages:
   - Stage 0: Exploring (curiosity)
   - Stage 1: Experimenting (pilot)
   - Stage 2: Implementing (first feature)
   - Stage 3: Scaling (multiple features)
   - Stage 4: Production (AI-native product)
3. How to move between stages
4. Common blockers at each stage
5. Resources for each stage

**Delivery:** Notion page or PDF (5 pages), no gate

**Creation time:** 4 hours

## Edge Cases & Warnings

### Warning 1: Don't Create Generic Lead Magnets
Your asset must be genuinely valuable, not just a gate to collect emails.
- ❌ "10 Tips for Better Marketing" (vague, already exists everywhere)
- ✅ "SaaS Pricing Page Teardown: 7 Patterns from $100M+ Companies" (specific, valuable)

### Warning 2: Un-gate When Possible
Lower friction = more distribution:
- No gate → Easy to share, builds trust faster
- Email-only gate → Reasonable for substantial assets (guides, case studies)
- Email + phone gate → Only for very high-value (avoid for cold outreach)

### Warning 3: Repurpose Existing Content
Don't start from scratch every time:
- Turn blog posts into guides
- Package frameworks you already use
- Document your actual process
- Curate resources you already recommend

### Warning 4: Keep It Fresh
Outdated assets hurt credibility:
- Update pricing/tool comparisons quarterly
- Add version numbers and "last updated" dates
- Archive old assets instead of sending stale content

## Quality Checks

Before offering an asset in outreach:

- [ ] Solves a specific, relevant problem for target persona
- [ ] Can be consumed in 15-30 minutes (for most assets)
- [ ] Includes concrete examples or templates (not just theory)
- [ ] Visually clean and professional (but doesn't need fancy design)
- [ ] Delivery is frictionless (1-2 clicks max)
- [ ] You'd genuinely find this valuable if you were the prospect
- [ ] Asset is current (updated in last 6 months)

## Asset Library Starter Pack

Build these 5 assets first (in order):

1. **Quick-Win Template** (2 hours)
   - Something they can use today
   - Solves a tactical problem
   - Example: "Outbound Email Subject Line Formula"

2. **Comparison/Framework** (3 hours)
   - Helps with a decision
   - Shows your expertise
   - Example: "5 AI Vendor Comparison Matrix"

3. **Core Guide** (6 hours)
   - Your signature methodology
   - Step-by-step walkthrough
   - Example: "30-Day Implementation Roadmap"

4. **Case Study** (4 hours)
   - Proof it works
   - Real numbers (with permission)
   - Example: "How Company X shipped AI in 60 days"

5. **Resource Collection** (2 hours)
   - Curated links + commentary
   - Saves them research time
   - Example: "15 Best AI/ML Resources for SaaS Teams"

**Total creation time: ~17 hours**
**Result: 5 assets covering multiple personas and use cases**
