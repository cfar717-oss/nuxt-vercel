## Purpose

Provide templates and frameworks for creating value-first outreach messages across three relationship stages: Cold (no prior connection), Warm (some shared context), and Hot (existing relationship or strong signal).

- **artifact.persona_map.v0_1**: Target persona archetype

- **artifact.asset_choice.v0_1**: Digital asset to offer
- **Relationship stage**: Cold, Warm, or Hot

## Outputs

- **artifact.outreach_sequence.v0_1**: Multi-touchpoint outreach campaign

## Process

### 1. Determine Relationship Stage

**Cold:**
- No prior connection
- Prospect doesn't know you
- No shared community or introduction

**Warm:**
- Shared connection, community, or event
- Prospect may have seen your content
- Some context exists

**Hot:**
- Prospect engaged with your content
- Explicit signal of interest
- Referral or direct request

### 2. Structure for Each Stage

**Cold Outreach Structure:**
```
1. Subject/Opener (60 chars max for email)
2. Context (1 sentence: why I'm reaching out)
3. Relevance (1 sentence: why you specifically)
4. Value offer (2-3 sentences: what I can give)
5. Permission-based CTA (1 sentence: invitation)
6. Easy out (optional: "no worries if not relevant")
```

**Warm Outreach Structure:**
```
1. Subject/Opener (reference shared context)
2. Shared connection (1 sentence)
3. Relevance (based on observable activity)
4. Value offer (more specific than cold)
5. Permission-based CTA
```

**Hot Outreach Structure:**
```
1. Subject/Opener (acknowledge their signal)
2. Reference their interest/question
3. Direct value delivery
4. Clear next step (still permission-based)
```

### 3. Apply Persona-Specific Tone

Adjust based on persona archetype (KB-002):

**Ambitious Builder → Direct and results-oriented**
- Focus on specific outcomes ("Save 10 hours/week")
- Short, punchy sentences
- CTA: Calendar link or clear yes/no

**Careful Optimizer → Analytical and detailed**
- Include data points or proof
- Slightly longer explanation okay
- CTA: "Want me to send the comparison spreadsheet?"

**Visionary Explorer → Big-picture and conceptual**
- Lead with the vision or trend
- Framework over tactics
- CTA: Open-ended question that invites dialogue

**Collaborative Connector → Warm and community-focused**
- Emphasize mutual benefit or community value
- More conversational tone
- CTA: Invitation to join or connect

### 4. Write Multi-Touch Sequence

Don't stop at one message. Plan 3-7 touchpoints:

**Touchpoint 1 (Day 0):** Intro + value demo
**Touchpoint 2 (Day 3):** Follow-up + asset offer
**Touchpoint 3 (Day 7):** Different angle + case example
**Touchpoint 4 (Day 14):** Gentle reminder + question
**Touchpoint 5 (Day 21):** Breakup email (permission to stop)

## Examples

### Example 1: Cold Outreach to "Ambitious Builder" Persona

**Prospect:** Sarah Chen, VP of Engineering at TechFlow (recently raised Series B)

**Email:**
```
Subject: Quick framework for hypergrowth hiring

Hi Sarah,

I saw TechFlow just raised $15M (congrats). Your post about maintaining culture while doubling the team really resonated.

I put together a 2-page framework on hypergrowth onboarding that cuts ramp time by ~40%. It's based on 12 companies that went from 20 → 100+ engineers.

If that sounds helpful, I'd be happy to send it over—no strings attached.

Either way, best of luck with the scaling!

Alex
```

**Why this works:**
- Subject is specific and outcome-focused (Ambitious Builder preference)
- Context is based on observable fact (Series B raise + LinkedIn post)
- Value is specific ("cuts ramp time by ~40%")
- Asset is actionable (framework, not theory)
- CTA is permission-based
- Easy out included

**Touchpoint 2 (Day 3):**
```
Subject: Re: Quick framework for hypergrowth hiring

Hi Sarah,

Quick follow-up—wanted to make sure my last note didn't get buried.

The framework covers:
- 30/60/90 day plans for senior vs junior hires
- Manager onboarding tracks
- Culture integration checkpoints

No obligation, just thought it might save you some headaches.

Want me to send it?

Alex
```

### Example 2: Warm Outreach to "Careful Optimizer" Persona

**Prospect:** Michael Torres, Director of Product (connected via SaaS Metrics community)

**LinkedIn DM:**
```
Hi Michael,

Saw your post in the SaaS Metrics group about A/B testing tools. I just finished a comparison of 7 tools (with pricing, feature matrix, and setup time estimates).

Since you mentioned evaluation criteria, thought you might find it useful.

Want me to DM you the spreadsheet? It's a Google Sheet you can copy/edit.

No pitch—just a resource.

— Jamie
```

**Why this works:**
- References shared community (warm context)
- Specific to his stated need (A/B testing tools)
- Offers data-driven asset (spreadsheet with numbers)
- Careful Optimizer loves comparisons
- No-pitch explicitly stated

### Example 3: Hot Outreach to "Visionary Explorer" Persona

**Prospect:** Lisa Park, CTO (replied to your Twitter thread on AI trends)

**Twitter DM:**
```
Hi Lisa,

Thanks for the thoughtful reply on the AI thread! Your point about "implementation gap > capability gap" is spot-on.

I'm working on a framework called "AI Readiness Ladder" that addresses exactly that. It's 5 stages from "exploring" to "production at scale."

Early draft here if you're curious: [link]

Would love your feedback if you have 10 mins—especially on the Stage 3 → 4 transition.

— Casey
```

**Why this works:**
- Acknowledges their contribution (shows you read it)
- Connects to big idea (Visionary Explorer preference)
- Invites collaboration (not just consumption)
- CTA is open-ended, invites dialogue
- Hot lead gets faster access to content

## Edge Cases & Warnings

### Warning 1: Don't Over-Personalize Cold Outreach
Mentioning too many specific details can feel creepy:
- ✅ "I saw your post about scaling challenges"
- ❌ "I noticed you tweeted 3 times about hiring in the last week and your LinkedIn says you joined TechFlow 14 months ago..."

### Warning 2: Sequence Timing Matters
Don't spam. Space touchpoints appropriately:
- Cold: 3-7 day gaps
- Warm: 2-5 day gaps
- Hot: 1-3 day gaps (they're already interested)

### Warning 3: Know When to Stop
If no response after 5 touchpoints → stop. Send a graceful breakup:
> "Haven't heard back, so I'll assume this isn't a priority right now. Feel free to reach out if circumstances change!"

### Warning 4: Match Channel to Persona
- Email: Works for everyone, best for longer content
- LinkedIn DM: Professional, good for B2B
- Twitter DM: More casual, good for tech/product folks
- Don't send cold DMs on Instagram for B2B SaaS

## Quality Checks

Before sending any outreach message:

- [ ] Subject line <= 60 characters (for email)
- [ ] Personalization based on observable facts only
- [ ] Value offer is specific and relevant to persona
- [ ] CTA is permission-based invitation
- [ ] No shame, fear, or pressure tactics
- [ ] No therapy/clinical language
- [ ] Passes Ethics & Integrity Gate
- [ ] Passes Deliverability Gate
